Community Action Project

14 Nov 2025

On 14 November 2025, our students proudly took part in a Citizenship Community Action Project focused on cleaning up Nyali Beach. This meaningful initiative reflects our school’s strong commitment to nurturing responsible, compassionate, and active members of the community.

Guided by their teachers, students worked together to collect waste, learn about environmental conservation, and understand the importance of protecting shared public spaces. Beyond cleaning the beach, the project encouraged teamwork, leadership, and a sense of personal responsibility. Students experienced first-hand how small actions can create a positive impact, reinforcing the values of citizenship taught in the classroom.

At our school, learning goes beyond academics. We believe that true education involves shaping character, empathy, and social awareness. Community projects like the Nyali Beach clean-up allow students to apply their knowledge in real-world contexts, helping them grow into confident individuals who care about their environment and society.

This project is a clear example of how our school prioritizes holistic development. By actively engaging students in community service, we prepare them not only for academic success but also for life as thoughtful, proactive, and socially responsible citizens.
